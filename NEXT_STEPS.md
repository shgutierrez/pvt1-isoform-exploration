# Next Phase

## Main Objective

Evaluate whether the PVT1 transcript families identified through structural and compositional analyses are represented in lymphoma datasets.

## Candidate Transcript Families

### Family A

- PVT1-272
- PVT1-313
- PVT1-296

### Family B

- PVT1-210
- PVT1-471

### Candidate Outliers

- PVT1-320
- PVT1-593
- PVT1-589

## Biological Hypothesis

PVT1 functional diversity may emerge from context-dependent selection of transcript families rather than from a single dominant transcript architecture.

## Planned Analyses

### Phase 1

- Identify publicly available Burkitt lymphoma RNA-seq datasets.
- Review metadata and cohort characteristics.

### Phase 2

- Evaluate global PVT1 expression.
- Determine whether transcript-level quantification is available.

### Phase 3

- Investigate representation of candidate transcript families.
- Compare transcript usage patterns across samples.

### Phase 4

- Explore associations with disease context.
- Evaluate potential links between transcript family usage and tumor biology.
